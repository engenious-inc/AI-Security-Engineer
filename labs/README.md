# Labs

Runnable lab targets for the hands-on sessions. Each lab is self-contained: clone the repo, `cd` into
the lab folder, and follow its README.

| Weekend | Lab | Used on |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | [`week2-mercibank/`](week2-mercibank/) — MerciBank Assistant, a deliberately-vulnerable banking agent (tools + RAG + memory) | Day 3 (recon & connect), Day 4 (attack & prove); target again in Weeks 3-4 |
| 3 | [`week3-attacker-agent/`](week3-attacker-agent/) — the Week 3 attacker agent, pointed at MerciBank | Day 5, Day 6 |
| 4 | [`attacker-pro/`](attacker-pro/) — RedCell, the advanced attacker agent with a dashboard, pointed at MerciBank | Day 7, Day 8 |
| 5 | [`../ai-security-chatbot/`](../ai-security-chatbot/) — NodeLine Tech, the ticketing-app target (Docker + Ollama); student guide, ROE and intake questionnaire in its `docs/` | Day 9, Day 10 |

> ⚠️ Everything here is **intentionally vulnerable** and built for authorized testing on your own
> machine. Never deploy a lab target, and never point your harness at a system you are not authorized
> to test.
